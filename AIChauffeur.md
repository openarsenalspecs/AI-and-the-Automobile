# Overview

AIChauffeur is an **open-source AI driving efficiency coach** designed to retrofit and enhance **ICE, hybrid, and electric vehicles** with intelligent efficiency optimization. The system uses artificial intelligence, vehicle data analysis, and modular integrations to help drivers reduce wasted energy, improve driving habits, and maximize vehicle performance.

AIChauffeur focuses on the principle that every vehicle can become more efficient through better decision-making. Instead of requiring vehicle replacement, AIChauffeur provides a retrofit-friendly intelligence layer that helps older and newer vehicles optimize braking, coasting, acceleration, energy usage, and operational efficiency.

The project combines **AI coaching, predictive analytics, regenerative optimization, and modular vehicle integrations** to create an adaptable platform for smarter transportation.
