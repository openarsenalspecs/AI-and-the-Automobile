# Crosswater

**No Owners. Just Builders.**

Crosswater is an open-source autonomous amphibious vehicle platform designed to operate seamlessly across land and water using electric propulsion, modular hardware architecture, and an open autonomy software stack.

It is built under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+)**, ensuring that all distributed, modified, or network-deployed versions remain open and contribute back to the community.

---

## 🌊 Project Vision

Crosswater exists to redefine mobility in environments where infrastructure is unstable, absent, or changing—such as flood zones, coastal regions, rural waterways, and disaster recovery areas.

It is not a product.  
It is a shared engineering platform.
