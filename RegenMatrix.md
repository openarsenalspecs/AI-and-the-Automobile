# RegenMatrix  
**Dynamic energy recovery for every road.**

---

## Summary

**RegenMatrix** is an **open-source AI-controlled regenerative tuning system** designed for **ICE, hybrid, and EV vehicles**. It combines **modular hardware units** with **AI-driven adaptive control** to maximize energy recovery, optimize efficiency, and provide actionable insights to drivers. The system **learns from driver behavior and road conditions** to reclaim wasted energy while remaining fully **retrofit-friendly and community-driven**.

---

## Master Feature List

### 1. Core AI & Driving Efficiency
- AI-controlled regenerative tuning for braking and drivetrain  
- Real-time energy recovery optimization  
- Driver behavior learning and profiling  
- Predictive regen for stops, turns, hills  
- Multi-driver profiles for shared vehicles  
- Weather- and road-condition adaptive regen  
- Reinforcement learning for adaptive braking  
- Hybrid AI-controller modes (eco, comfort, aggressive)  
- Self-calibrating AI modules  
- Predictive battery and power management  

### 2. Modular Hardware Features
- Plug-and-play modular units for easy retrofit  
- Integration with mechanical and electronic braking systems  
- Suspension energy harvesting  
- Micro-turbine or alternator units for airflow or drivetrain energy  
- Thermal energy recovery from engine or battery systems  
- Accessory load management for lights, climate, and electronics  
- Kinetic energy capture from door and suspension movement  
- Vehicle body vibration energy harvesting  
- Solar or auxiliary micro-generation integration  
- Dynamic braking torque allocation  
- Adaptive thermal recovery for fluids and cabin  

### 3. Vehicle & Fleet Optimization
- Fleet-level AI learning for multi-vehicle optimization  
- Vehicle-to-vehicle energy coordination for fleets or convoys  
- Route-aware regen using GPS, elevation, and traffic data  
- Eco-driving gamification with points or efficiency scoring  
- Fleet predictive maintenance  
- Shared AI learning across vehicles  
- AI-driven convoy energy optimization  
- Energy marketplace integration (V2G or grid interaction)  

### 4. Simulation & Testing
- Virtual vehicle simulator for ICE, hybrid, and EV models  
- Trip replay & scenario testing for AI tuning validation  
- Open benchmark datasets for AI training  
- Energy KPI visualization (braking efficiency, throttle smoothness, regen capture, energy recovered)  
- Simulation SDK for developers  
- Automated testing pipelines for AI models and firmware  

### 5. User Feedback & Experience
- HUD or phone display dashboards for real-time energy recovery  
- Haptic feedback via pedal or steering wheel cues  
- Optional AI voice coaching for efficient regen use  
- Adaptive notifications triggered only for meaningful improvements  
- AR HUD overlays for visualizing regen data  
- Driver coaching AI avatars  
- Eco-driving challenges and achievements  
- Gamified leaderboards for drivers or fleets  

### 6. Analytics & Visualization
- Predictive dashboards for upcoming trips  
- Comparative trip analytics across drivers, times, or routes  
- Heatmaps of regen efficiency by road or driving pattern  
- Integration with smart city dashboards for traffic optimization  
- Fleet-wide energy analytics and performance reporting  

### 7. Open-Source & Community
- Modular plugin architecture for AI models, sensors, or hardware expansion  
- Open data export formats (CSV/JSON) for trips, energy, and AI feedback  
- Comprehensive documentation and step-by-step retrofit guides  
- Community roadmap and feature voting system  
- Cross-platform support (ICE, hybrid, EV)  
- AI model marketplace for contributor-shared models  
- Modular sensor expansion and plug-ins

## Usage

1. **Hardware Installation:**  
   - Install modular regen units per vehicle type.  
   - Connect to braking, drivetrain, and accessory systems.  

2. **Software Setup:**  
   - Clone this repository:  
     ```bash
     git clone https://gitlab.com/Roxanne_Ardary/regenmatrix.git
     ```  
   - Follow the setup instructions in `/docs/setup.md`  
   - Run AI calibration for your specific vehicle model.  

3. **Operation:**  
   - AI adapts in real-time to driver behavior, route, and traffic conditions.  
   - Optional HUD, haptic, or voice feedback can be enabled.  
   - Track energy recovery metrics via dashboards or exported datasets.
